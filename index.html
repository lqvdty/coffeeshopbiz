How to read this

Owner salary versus investment profit

Turning employees off removes their salary and employer contribution, but does not make your work free. Enter the full company cost of your own compensation to distinguish labour income from investment profit. Additional coverage starts at zero and must be budgeted separately.

Cash, reserves and accounting

This is a planning surplus with repair/replacement reserves. Reserves are not necessarily monthly accounting expenses. Depreciation is not calculated. Loan principal is shown as a cash payment, not a profit expense. Corporate tax applies here to a simplified positive surplus before reserves and loan principal; actual taxable profit also requires depreciation and tax adjustments.

Hungarian tax regimes

Standard employer contribution starts at 13%, matching our earlier model. If the company uses KIVA, do not stack standard employer tax and corporate tax on top. Set the employer percentage appropriately and enter a separate accountant-provided monthly tax allowance. Local business tax and tourism contribution cannot be determined simply from the final profit.

What is excluded unless you enter it?

Food sales and inventory, delivery-platform commissions, additional staffing, paid cleaners, owner compensation, music/terrace/signage fees, recruitment, legal costs, financing, personal dividend taxes, major refurbishment and unknown liabilities. Use additional overhead, coverage, upfront-capital and tax fields. There is no forecast of seasonality or annual growth.

Public pricing context

Bookkeeping and payroll are working budget inputs; get a café-specific quote. Card default is 80% card share at a 1.95% fee. See SumUp Hungary standard fees, bookkeeping price examples, and Hungarian corporate-tax overview. Rates and applicability must be checked.
